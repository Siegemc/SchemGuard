# Schematic Guard — Privacy Policy

Schematic Guard processes the information needed to run its commands, including your Discord user ID, server information, uploaded schematic filename, and the file itself. This information is used only to scan the schematic and provide a report or cleaned copy.

Uploaded files are temporarily held in server memory and normally expire after approximately 15 minutes or when the bot restarts. Limited technical logs, such as command errors, timestamps, or filenames, may be retained for troubleshooting and security. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Information may be processed by Discord and the bot’s hosting provider as required to operate the service. Do not upload sensitive personal information. You may contact the Siege Staff Department through its Discord support channel with privacy questions or requests.
